# Weapon Shop Empire - Employee Assignment Prototype

## Current Version: v0.2.01

Standalone HTML prototype for assigning workers to Sales, Crafting, Expedition, or the Staff House.

## Open and Test

Open `Weapon_Shop_Empire_Employee_Assignment_v0.2.01.html` in a modern browser.

## Controls

- Click a worker to inspect SELL, CRAFT, ATK, and DEF.
- Use Auto Assign for the recommended department.
- Use the assignment buttons and Confirm.
- On desktop, drag a worker into a department zone.
- As an alternative, select a worker and double-click a department.

## Staff House Rules

- Staff House replaces the previous generic Rest assignment.
- Healthy unassigned workers are Available and receive 50% wage.
- Workers injured during monster expeditions recover HP in Staff House and receive 75% wage.
- Sales and Crafting do not normally consume HP.
- Active workers receive 100% wage.
- Expeditions may add hazard pay.
